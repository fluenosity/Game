## Vision Statement

**`PROPOSED`**

> A 1–5 player cooperative survival horror game on Roblox where fire is your only defense against a learning, adaptive monster. Survive five nights in an abandoned forest shelter by gathering resources, building defenses, and uncovering the truth behind the creature that destroyed a city. Every night the fire burns lower. Every night the monster gets braver.

**`PROPOSED`**

## Design Pillars

**`PROPOSED`** — These guide every design decision. If a feature doesn't serve at least one pillar, it doesn't belong in the game.

### Pillar 1: Fire Is Life

Fire is not a passive safety net — it is a **living, depleting resource** that demands constant attention. The campfire slowly dies. Players must actively feed it, upgrade it, and decide how to allocate fuel. The tension between needing fire and running out of fuel is the emotional core of every night.

**Design test:** Does this feature make fire management more interesting? Does it create meaningful decisions about fuel?

### Pillar 2: The Monster Is Memorable

The monster is not a generic threat — it has a recognizable **identity** (visual, audio, behavioral) that players remember, fear, discuss, and share. It learns from player behavior. It tests defenses. It has personality. Players should feel like they are being **hunted by something intelligent**, not just avoiding a damage hitbox.

**Design test:** Would a player describe this monster to a friend? Does its behavior create stories worth telling?

### Pillar 3: Every Decision Matters

Resources are scarce. Time is limited. Players must constantly choose: explore further or return to safety? Upgrade the fire or stockpile fuel? Trade for better materials or save what you have? There are no objectively correct answers — only tradeoffs that create tension and replayability.

**Design test:** Does this feature create a meaningful choice, or is there an obvious best option?

### Pillar 4: Cooperative Tension

Playing with friends should be more intense, not easier. Co-op creates **social tension**: who guards the fire? Who ventures into the dark? What happens when someone doesn't come back? Players should need each other, argue about strategy, and experience moments of panic and relief together.

**Design test:** Does this feature work differently (not just "more easily") with 2–5 players? Does it create cooperative moments?

### Pillar 5: Show, Don't Tell

The game teaches through experience, not tutorials. The cutscene shows the monster fearing fire — no text explains it. The forest path leads to the shelter — no waypoint is needed. Lore is discovered through environmental clues — not delivered through exposition dumps. Players feel smart when they figure things out.

**Design test:** Can a player understand this without reading instructions? Does discovery feel rewarding?

## Target Experience

### Emotional Arc Per Session

```
CURIOSITY → UNDERSTANDING → CONFIDENCE → DISRUPTION → PANIC → RELIEF → ACCOMPLISHMENT
(Day start)   (Learn tasks)   (Prepared)   (Night falls) (Monster) (Dawn) (Survived)
```

### Target Emotions by Day

| Day | Primary Emotion | Secondary Emotion |
|-----|----------------|-------------------|
| Day 0 (Prologue) | Awe / Fear | Curiosity |
| Day 1 | Vulnerability / Urgency | Relief (survived first night) |
| Day 2 | Growing competence | Anxiety (fire burns out faster) |
| Day 3 | Exploration excitement | Greed vs. safety tension |
| Day 4 | Dread / Overwhelm | Determination |
| Day 5 | Desperate hope | Triumph or devastating loss |

### What Players Should Feel After Playing

- "That monster is terrifying."
- "The moment the fire almost went out was insane."
- "We barely made it back before nightfall."
- "I want to try that again with a different strategy."
- "I need to show my friends this game."

### What Players Should NOT Feel

- "This is just another survival game."
- "I don't know what I'm supposed to do."
- "Nothing interesting happened."
- "The monster is just annoying, not scary."
- "My phone can't run this."

## Target Audience

### Primary: Roblox Horror Enthusiasts (Ages 10–17)

- Players who enjoy DOORS, The Mimic, Pressure, 99 Nights in the Forest
- Seek tension, jumpscares, cooperative fear
- Share clips on TikTok / YouTube
- Play primarily on mobile

### Secondary: Survival Game Fans (Ages 13–24)

- Players who enjoy base building, resource management, progression
- Seek strategic depth, meaningful upgrades
- Play in longer sessions
- More likely on PC

### Tertiary: Content Creators

- YouTubers and streamers who cover Roblox horror
- Need "clip-worthy" moments
- Their content drives organic growth

---

## What This Game Is NOT

- Not an open-world sandbox (it has a focused 5-day campaign)
- Not a jumpscare-only horror experience (tension > sudden scares)
- Not a PvP game (cooperative or solo only)
- Not a simulator or idle game
- Not a pay-to-win game
- Not a clone of 99 Nights in the Forest, DOORS, or The Mimic
