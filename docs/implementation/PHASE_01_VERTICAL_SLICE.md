## 1. Phase 1 Objective: "The Polished First Impression"

The Vertical Slice is a fully realized, production-quality implementation of the opening 15 minutes of the game: **Day 0 Prologue through Day 1 Dawn**. It serves as the primary evaluation milestone to prove market viability, visual wow-factor, and player retention.

---

## 2. Scope & Inclusions

1. **Day 0 Prologue:**
   - Cinematic sequence of ruined city aftermath.
   - The monster ("The Hollow") attacks, encounters a burst of fire, and shrieks in retreat.
   - Seamless camera transition into playable first-person escape down the forest trail.
2. **Day 1 Full Survival Experience:**
   - Polished pine forest environment with custom lighting, atmospheric volumetric fog, and ambient wind audio.
   - Fully textured shelter compound with damaged fence, old sofa, and stone campfire.
   - Production animations for wood chopping, carrying logs, and stoking fire.
   - The Hollow custom 3D model with sound language (breathing, clicking, roar).
   - Night survival with dynamic fuel burn and light boundary pacing.
   - Dawn transition with sofa sleep interaction and morning debrief.
3. **Multiplayer & Platform Support:**
   - 1 to 5 player lobby matchmaking via the 5 tents.
   - Verified 30+ FPS performance on low-end mobile devices.
   - Touchscreen virtual thumbsticks and custom interaction buttons.

---

## 3. Exit Criteria (Gate to Full Campaign Phase 2)

- [ ] **EX-01-1:** Complete Day 0 -> Day 1 playthrough runs end-to-end with zero placeholder graybox assets.
- [ ] **EX-01-2:** Blind playtesters rate the opening prologue and first night atmosphere as >= 4.0 / 5.0 in horror tension.
- [ ] **EX-01-3:** Mobile devices achieve >= 30 FPS across entire vertical slice sequence.
- [ ] **EX-01-4:** 5-player cooperative session successfully shares resources and completes Day 1 without disconnect softlocks.
