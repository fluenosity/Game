## 1. Phase 2 Objective: "The Complete Narrative Arc"

Phase 2 builds out the complete 5-day survival progression following approval of the Vertical Slice. It integrates all advanced gameplay systems—scavenging, trading, weather events, base upgrades, and the climactic signal tower escape.

---

## 2. Daily Implementation Milestones

### Milestone 2.1: Day 2 — Fire Upgrades & Weather
- Implementation of Campfire Tier 2 & Tier 3 upgrades.
- Introduction of Dry Hardwood fuel and rainstorm weather event.
- Shelter canopy roof upgrade preventing rain fuel penalty.

### Milestone 2.2: Day 3 — Scavenging & Silas the Trader
- Spawning of salvage resource nodes (Metal Scrap, Plastic Bottles, Rope).
- Workbench crafting station (Hatchet upgrades, storage chest expansion, flares).
- Silas the Wandering Trader arrival, schedule, barter UI, and stock limits.

### Milestone 2.3: Day 4 — Escalation & Light Network
- The Hollow AI escalation: probing weak fence sections and reacting to noise.
- Connected Light Network: Relay Posts, Perimeter Lanterns, and line-of-sight raycasts.
- Gale wind weather and auditory muffling.

### Milestone 2.4: Day 5 — The Signal & Escape
- Signal Tower multi-part repair quest (Battery, Transmitter, Fuel Cell).
- Final night defense encounter with escalating monster waves.
- Dawn arrival, evacuation transport landing, and 4 narrative ending cutscenes.

---

## 3. Exit Criteria (Gate to Polish Phase 3)

- [ ] **EX-02-1:** Complete 5-day campaign can be played from start to finish without developer cheats or manual intervention.
- [ ] **EX-02-2:** All 4 narrative endings trigger under their respective conditions with correct badges awarded.
- [ ] **EX-02-3:** Silas the Trader barter transactions are 100% server-authoritative and exploit-proof.
- [ ] **EX-02-4:** Full campaign duration across 5 days lands within 45–65 minutes median playtime.
