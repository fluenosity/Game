## What's Trending in Roblox Horror (2026)

1. **Clear survival mechanics** — not just jumpscares.
2. **Teamwork / co-op** — social tension over solo grinding.
3. **Atmosphere & sound design** — lighting and audio over gore.
4. **Narrative & lore** — mystery drives YouTube content.
5. **Low-friction entry** — players understand the game in 10 seconds.
6. **Frequent updates** — 2–4 week content cycles.

# Design Pillars

> See [01_GAME_VISION.md](../01_GAME_VISION.md) for full pillar definitions.

1. **Fire Is Life** — Fire is an active, depleting resource that demands attention.
2. **The Monster Is Memorable** — Distinctive identity, intelligent behavior, emotional resonance.
3. **Every Decision Matters** — Scarce resources, meaningful tradeoffs, no obvious best strategy.
4. **Cooperative Tension** — Co-op adds social stress, not just more hands.
5. **Show, Don't Tell** — Teach through environment, not tutorials.

# Differentiation Strategy

## How We Stand Out From 99 Nights and Others

| Differentiator | Description | Competitor Gap |
|---------------|-------------|---------------|
| **The Hollow** | A named, designed, emotionally complex monster with unique visual/audio identity | 99 Nights' "Deer" is simpler; most Roblox monsters are generic |
| **Active fire management** | Fire depletes, shrinks, can go out mid-night. Player must actively feed it. | Most games: light fire = safe. Ours: light fire = start of the work |
| **Monster learns** | The Hollow adapts its behavior across nights, testing defenses, finding weaknesses | Rare in Roblox — most monsters follow fixed patterns |
| **5-day focused campaign** | Complete narrative arc with a defined ending, not endless survival | 99 Nights is 99 nights (long grind); ours is a focused experience |
| **Lore with emotional depth** | The Hollow is tragic, not just evil. Discovering why creates empathy + fear | Most Roblox horror lacks emotional depth |
| **Clip-worthy fire moments** | Fire flickering, almost dying, monster circling — designed for TikTok | Deliberate "shareable moment" design |

## The One-Line Pitch

> *"The fire is dying. The monster is learning. How many nights can you last?"*
