## 1. The Four Non-Negotiable Pillars

Every mechanic, narrative beat, and candidate feature must be evaluated against these four core design pillars. If a proposed feature violates any pillar, it must be rejected or redesigned.

## 2. Pillar Breakdown & Design Tests

### Pillar 1: Show, Don't Tell (Environmental Storytelling)
- **Principle:** The world reveals its horror through visual clues, sound design, and tangible objects—not through mandatory walls of text or long dialogue dumps.
- **The Design Test:** *"Can a player understand what happened and what to do purely by observing the environment and following visual/audio feedback?"*

### Pillar 2: Fire is Life (Active Resource Protection)
- **Principle:** Fire is not a passive decorative campfire. It is the sole barrier between humanity and an ancient, light-fearing predator. Fuel depletes actively, requiring vigilance and sacrifice.
- **The Design Test:** *"Does this mechanic heighten the player's emotional relationship with maintaining and protecting light?"*

### Pillar 3: Earned Survival (Skill, Triage & Preparation)
- **Principle:** Surviving the night must feel earned through daytime preparation, resource discipline, and clutch teamwork—not random dice rolls or artificial sponge health bars.
- **The Design Test:** *"When a player dies, do they understand their mistake and feel motivated to try a smarter survival strategy next time?"*

### Pillar 4: Ethical Horror & Pure Experience (Zero Pay-to-Win)
- **Principle:** Horror dissolves the moment players can purchase invincibility, infinite fuel, or instant revives with Robux. All monetization is strictly ethical and cosmetic.
- **The Design Test:** *"Does this feature compromise the vulnerability and parity of players who do not spend money?"*
