## 2. Purpose & Intended Emotion

### Purpose

**Disrupt everything the player thought they knew.** The monster changes behavior. The rules shift. What worked before may not work tonight.

Day 4 is the "everything goes wrong" day — the second-act crisis.

### Target Emotions

| Phase | Emotion |
|-------|---------|
| Morning | Unease ("Something feels different") |
| Exploration | Discovery + growing dread |
| Finding signal components | Hope mixed with urgency |
| Night 4 | Terror (the monster does something NEW) |
| Surviving Night 4 | Exhausted relief, determination |

## 3. Narrative Context

The player has been surviving for three nights. They've built up their shelter, gathered resources, maybe even found lore clues. They feel competent.

**Day 4 destroys that confidence.**

The monster has been watching. Learning. It knows the player's patterns. Tonight, it changes tactics.

## 7. New Mechanics Introduced

### 7.1 Monster Escalation `PROPOSED`

**The monster learns.** Specific changes on Night 4:

| Behavior | Nights 1–3 | Night 4 |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| Fire avoidance | Absolute — never enters radius | `PROPOSED` Can briefly tolerate Tier 1 fire radius (5s exposure) |
| Approach pattern | Predictable, from ground level | `PROPOSED` Approaches from unexpected angles |
| Fence interaction | Rattling (Day 3) | `PROPOSED` Can BREAK a section of unrepaired fence |
| Sound | Growling, footsteps | `PROPOSED` Mimics player sounds (false footsteps, fake branch snaps) |
| Intelligence | Patrols and tests | `PROPOSED` Targets the weakest section of the shelter |

**Key design rule:** The monster's new behavior must be UNDERSTANDABLE. The player should be scared but should also be able to figure out the new rules and adapt.

### 7.2 Signal Tower Discovery `PROPOSED`

Deep in the forest (furthest exploration point), the player finds:
- An old, partially destroyed radio tower.
- A note: *"If you can find the parts, you can send a signal. They'll come for you."*
- A list of 3 components needed to activate it (found across Days 4–5).

**Signal components (Day 4 availability):**

| Component | Location | Found on Day 4? |
|-----------|----------|-----------------|
| Battery Pack | Inside the ruined shack (Landmark B) | ✅ Yes |
| Antenna Wire | Deep forest, new area past landmarks | ✅ Yes |
| Radio Module | `PROPOSED` Guarded location near monster path | Day 5 only |

The player can find 2 of 3 components on Day 4, setting up Day 5's final push.

### 7.3 Environmental Hazards `PROPOSED`

| Hazard | Effect | Countermeasure |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| Strong wind | `PROPOSED` Reduces fire intensity temporarily | Add extra fuel, shelter walls |
| Rain (brief) | `PROPOSED` Slows fire burning, reduces visibility | Cover fire (upgrade), wait it out |
| Darkness patches | `PROPOSED` Areas of the forest that are darker than normal | Torch required |

**Design rule:** Hazards are **predictable events, not random punishments.** The player sees the wind picking up, hears the rain coming, and can prepare.

## 8. Step-by-Step Gameplay Flow

### Step 1: Morning — Ominous Start (0:00–1:00)

- "DAY 4" appears.
- `PROPOSED` The morning feels different: fog lingers longer, sky is overcast, ambient audio is muted.
- Something is visibly wrong at the shelter: scratch marks on the fence that weren't there before.
- Objective: **"Prepare for tonight."** (deliberately vague — creates unease)

### Step 2: Exploration — Deeper Forest (1:00–5:00)

- Player must venture further than ever before.
- New area accessible: a deeper section of forest with denser trees, less light.
- `PROPOSED` Signal tower discovered — sets up the escape objective.
- Signal components scattered — player collects Battery Pack and Antenna Wire.
- More lore items: *"It's learning. Every night it watches longer before it moves. Don't let the fire get small."*

### Step 3: Trader Visit #2 (3:00–5:00)

`PROPOSED` The trader returns with upgraded offerings:

| Player Gives | Player Gets |
|-------------|-------------|
| 5 Metal Scrap + 3 Rope | Reinforced Fuel (burns 3x slower) |
| 4 Plastic + 2 Metal | Emergency Flare (instant relight, one use) |
| 6 Metal + 4 Rope | Campfire Tier 3 Upgrade Kit |

The emergency flare is critical — it's the player's safety net if the fire goes out on Night 4.

### Step 4: Preparation (5:00–6:00)

- Player returns to shelter.
- Upgrades campfire (if possible).
- Repairs fence (if smart — the monster will test it tonight).
- Loads fuel.
- Places torches at strategic points.
- Stores extra items.

### Step 5: Night 4 — Everything Changes (6:00–12:00)

**Night 4 is 360 seconds (6 minutes real-time) — the longest non-final night.**

**Phase A: Quiet Start (0–120s)**
- Silence. No monster sounds. Uncomfortably quiet.
- The player waits. Nothing happens. Tension builds through absence.

**Phase B: The Test (120–180s)**
- `PROPOSED` The monster approaches rapidly — faster than before.
- It reaches the protection radius and PAUSES.
- It doesn't retreat immediately. It stands at the edge. Watching.
- `PROPOSED` If the campfire is Tier 1, the monster steps INSIDE the radius for 5 seconds before retreating.
- The message is clear: **Tier 1 fire is no longer fully safe.**

**Phase C: The Attack (180–300s)**
- `PROPOSED` The monster circles to the weakest section of fence.
- If the fence is unrepaired: it breaks through, entering the yard (but staying outside fire radius).
- If the fence is repaired: it rattles and damages but doesn't break through.
- The monster is now INSIDE the shelter perimeter but still avoiding the fire.
- Audio: heavy breathing, scraping, the fence groaning.

**Phase D: The Siege (300–360s)**
- The monster makes one final, aggressive approach.
- `PROPOSED` If fire is Tier 2+, it retreats as normal.
- `PROPOSED` If fire is Tier 1, it pushes into the radius again — longer this time (10s).
- Dawn begins. Monster retreats. But it was CLOSE.

### Step 6: Dawn & Day 4 End

- Dawn. Monster flees (dramatic retreat — faster, angrier than before).
- Player sleeps → "DAY 5"
- Remaining signal components noted: "You still need the Radio Module."

## 12. Monster Behavior (Day 4) — State Machine

| State | Entry | Behavior | Exit |
|-------|-------|----------|------|
| DORMANT | Day | Not present | → SILENT_APPROACH at nightfall |
| SILENT_APPROACH | Night begins | Approaches without sound for 120s | → OBSERVE |
| OBSERVE | Reaches radius edge | Stands still, watches for 30–60s | → CIRCLE |
| CIRCLE | After observation | Moves around shelter, looking for weakness | → BREACH_ATTEMPT |
| BREACH_ATTEMPT | Finds weak fence | Attacks fence section | → YARD_PATROL (if breach) or → CIRCLE (if repelled) |
| YARD_PATROL | Inside fence | Circles inside yard, outside fire radius | → FIRE_TEST |
| FIRE_TEST | Approaches fire | `PROPOSED` Briefly enters Tier 1 radius | → RETREAT or → ATTACK |
| RETREAT | Fire Tier 2+ or day | Backs away, patrols | → CIRCLE or → DAWN_RETREAT |
| ATTACK | Fire out or player exposed | Charges player | → RETREAT (if fire relit) |
| DAWN_RETREAT | Dawn signal | Aggressive flee | → DORMANT |
